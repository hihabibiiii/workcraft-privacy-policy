WorkCraft Account and Data Deletion

App: WorkCraft – Smart Work Management
Last updated: 6 November 2026

WorkCraft users can request deletion of their account and associated data by following the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to hshabibullah@gmail.com from the email address associated with your WorkCraft account.
  2. Use the subject line WorkCraft Account Deletion Request.
  3. In your message, state that you want your WorkCraft account and associated data deleted. Include your registered account email address.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What data may be deleted

After your request is verified, we will delete your WorkCraft account and associated data that we control, which may include account information, worker and work-site records, attendance, wage and payment records, contract information, client details and client-payment records.

Data that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained where required by applicable law, for legitimate accounting or record-keeping purposes, to resolve disputes, or to protect the security of the service. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Subscriptions and Google Play

Deleting your WorkCraft account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play. To avoid future subscription charges, manage or cancel your subscription separately in Google Play.

Need help?

For account deletion questions, contact hshabibullah@gmail.com.

For information about how WorkCraft handles personal data, read our Privacy Policy.
