Privacy Policy

App: WorkCraft – Smart Work Management
Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WorkCraft (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the WorkCraft mobile application.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, WorkCraft may process information that you enter into the app, including:

  • Account and authentication information used to sign in.
  • Worker and work-site details, such as names and site information.
  • Attendance records, work days, wage details and payment records.
  • Contract, client and client-payment information that you choose to record.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your use of the app and you are authorised to do so.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide the app’s core functions, including account access, worker and site management, attendance tracking, wage and payment tracking, contract and client-payment management, data synchronisation, app security, troubleshooting and support.

3. Storage and service providers

WorkCraft uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for account authentication and cloud-based data storage and synchronisation. The app may also use local storage on your device to support app functionality.

Firebase is provided by Google. Information processed by Google services is subject to Google’s applicable privacy terms and safeguards. WorkCraft does not ask for or store your full payment-card details for Google Play purchases.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

WorkCraft may offer paid subscriptions through Google Play. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play under its own payment and privacy processes. WorkCraft may receive subscription status and related purchase information needed to provide and verify subscription features.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support app functionality, such as Google Firebase and Google Play, or disclosed where required by applicable law. Information you enter about workers, clients or other people may be accessible to people who have access to your WorkCraft account or device.

6. Data security

We use platform and service-provider security features intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Keep your account credentials confidential and secure your device.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain your account and records, meet applicable legal requirements, and resolve disputes. To request account or associated data deletion, email us from your registered email address. We may need to verify your request. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping.

8. Children’s privacy

WorkCraft is a work-management tool and is not designed specifically for children. It should be used by adults managing work-related records. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
